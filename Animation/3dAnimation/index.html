Gesture guide
  • Drag — pinch thumb + index on the robot, move, open fingers to drop.
  • Rotate — pinch and move: left/right = Y, up/down = X. Tilt your wrist (like turning a knob) while pinching = Z.
  • Scale — pinch with both hands, move them apart (bigger) or together (smaller).
  • Mouse/touch: drag the robot to move or rotate; drag empty space to orbit the camera; wheel scales in Scale mode.
